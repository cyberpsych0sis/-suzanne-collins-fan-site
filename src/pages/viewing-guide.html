The 60-Second Watch Order

Watch in release order. The prequel assumes you already know who Snow becomes — so save it for last, then rewind to the beginning of Panem’s story if you want it chronologically.

  1. 01

    The Hunger Games

    2012 · 2h 22m · the 74th Games

    The perfect entry point — everything is explained as it happens.

  2. 02

    Catching Fire

    2013 · 2h 11m · the 75th Games

    Picks up one year later — the arena gets deadlier, the stakes get political.

  3. 03

    Mockingjay – Part 1

    2014 · 2h 3m · war begins

    No arena this time — it’s the rebellion’s slow, tense build-up.

  4. 04

    Mockingjay – Part 2

    2015 · 2h 17m · the finale

    The climax of Katniss’s story — watch this before the prequel for full impact.

  5. 05

    Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes

    2023 · 2h 37m · the 10th Games

    A prequel about young Snow — hits hardest once you know the president he becomes.

Marathon tip: the four Katniss films run about 9 hours total. Splitting them over two evenings — two films, a break, then the finale plus the prequel — keeps the arc easy to follow without any reading at all.

The Hunger Games

In the ruins of a future North America, the Capitol forces each district to send two teenagers to fight to the death on live television. When Katniss Everdeen volunteers in her sister’s place, her survival — and her defiance — become must-see TV for a nation that has never seen anything like it.

Catching Fire

Katniss and Peeta are champions now — and the Capitol wants them back. For the 75th Games, a Quarter Quell rounds up past victors, while unrest spreads through the districts. The arena is built to finish what the first Games started.

Mockingjay – Part 1

The war for Panem begins in the shadows. Katniss is rescued into District 13, where the rebels want her to become the Mockingjay — the symbol that can unite a fractured nation. It’s the quiet, tense half of the finale, and Part 2 needs it.

Mockingjay – Part 2

The rebellion marches on the Capitol, and the final battle turns the city itself into the arena. Katniss must decide what the victory is worth — and what she’s willing to become to win it.

The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes

Sixty-four years before Katniss, a young Coriolanus Snow mentors Lucy Gray Baird in the 10th Games — a scrappy, barely-watched contest still finding its feet. Every choice he makes here is the first draft of the president Panem would come to fear.

Books only

No films (yet) for the rest

The Underland Chronicles and the picture books have no screen adaptations, so the movies above are the complete Collins viewing experience for now. If you want those worlds too, start with Collections — or keep watching Panem with the Map of Panem open beside you.
